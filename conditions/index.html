Information document for exercising the right of withdrawal provided for in Article L.112-10 of the French Insurance Code.

You have acquired or are about to acquire a bicycle and wish to insure against the risks of theft and damage. The "Bikelp SLP Bicycle Insurance" contract addresses this need and provides suitable coverage.

Important Information

You are advised to verify that you do not already benefit from coverage covering any of the risks insured under the new policy. If you do, you have the right to withdraw from this policy within fourteen (calendar) days from its conclusion, without fee or penalty, provided that all the following conditions are satisfied:

  • you entered into this contract for non-professional purposes;
  • this contract complements the purchase of goods or a service provided by a supplier;
  • you demonstrate that you are already covered for one of the risks insured under this new contract;
  • the policy you wish to renounce has not been fully performed;
  • you have not reported any claim covered under this contract.

In such circumstances, you may exercise your right of withdrawal by letter or any other durable medium addressed to the insurer of the new policy, accompanied by documentation proving that you already hold coverage for one of the risks insured under the new policy. The insurer must reimburse the premium paid within thirty days from your withdrawal.

If you wish to cancel your contract but do not meet all of the above conditions, please review the cancellation terms set out in your contract.

Definitions

Certain terms have a specific meaning when appearing in bold type in this document and in Your Specific Terms. These terms are defined below.

  • Abandoned: When Your Insured Equipment is left unattended outside Your Home for a duration exceeding that specified in Your Specific Terms.
  • Accessories: Clothing and/or other items connected with the use of Your Bicycle.
  • Accident: A sudden, unintended, and unforeseen event, including acts of vandalism and malicious mischief.
  • Lock: A locking device meeting the security standard specified in Your Specific Terms.
  • Insurer: Occidente Seguros.
  • Community: Our customers.
  • Specific Terms: The specific terms and schedule of Your policy issued to You.
  • Race: Any mass-start event during which You use Your Insured Equipment and:
    • for which You must hold a valid sporting licence to participate;
    • that the event organiser designates as a competitive race;
    • with a ranking determined by timed results or competitive scoring;
    • that is not a Professional Race.
  • Professional Race: Any event for which You receive financial compensation or an appearance salary for Your participation.
  • Home: The address stated in Your Specific Terms and any private locked storage unit.
  • Damage: Physical loss, destruction, or damage caused by an accident that prevents the use of the property or impairs its core function.
  • Cyber Damage: Damage caused by software updates, viruses, malware, or any unauthorised, malicious, or criminal acts or series of acts involving the use, operation of, or access to any electronic device or computer system, or the threat or hoax thereof.
  • Cosmetic Damage: Scuffs, scratches, dents, or superficial alterations to an item of Insured Equipment that do not prevent the use or primary function of the item, but impair secondary attributes such as aesthetics or ergonomics.
  • Insured Equipment: The Bicycle(s) and accessories specified in Your Specific Terms.
  • Plan: The product package and policy options listed in Your Specific Terms.
  • Deductible: The initial sum payable by You towards any claim, or the amount deducted from any claim settlement, or the minimum claim threshold as stated in Your Specific Terms.
  • War: All consequences arising out of war, invasion, acts of foreign enemies, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military coup, or usurpation of power.
  • Bikelp SLP: Bikelp SLP.
  • Private Storage Unit: A secure, locked space to which access is controlled exclusively by You.
  • We / Us / Our: Bikelp SLP acting as an appointed agent of the Insurer.
  • Bikelp SLP Platform: The online platform where You can view policy details, manage adjustments, and lodge insurance claims.
  • Proof of Ownership: For any item of Insured Equipment or a Lock: We accept photographs clearly showing the make and model of the Bicycle as well as every component in the condition it was in when added to Your policy, as valid proof of ownership. We may also, at Our discretion, request supplementary proof at the time of claim, such as the original purchase receipt.
  • Premium: The monthly sum You pay reflecting Your proportion of the Community's claims costs for the previous month, including Bikelp SLP administrative fees and applicable statutory insurance taxes. Your monthly premium will never exceed the Capped Monthly Premium specified in Your Specific Terms.
  • Capped Monthly Premium: The maximum Premium You will be required to pay for any single month of coverage, as stated in Your Specific Terms.
  • Bicycle(s): Any two-wheeled pedal cycle, adult tricycle, or tandem propelled by human muscle power via pedals, specified in Your Specific Terms. This definition includes electrically power assisted cycles (EPACs):
    • equipped with pedal assistance;
    • requiring pedalling to initiate propulsion;
    • where motor assistance cuts out at or above 25 km/h;
    • possessing a maximum rated continuous power output of 250 watts.
  • Clothing: Any garments or technical apparel You wear while operating the Bicycle.
  • You / Your: The insured person named in Your Specific Terms and family members permanently residing at their Home.

2. Which law governs this policy?

3. When does coverage take effect?

Your coverage begins when We issue Your Specific Terms, starting on the effective inception date stated therein.

4. How is the Premium paid?

4.1. Your Premium consists of two components:

(a) Your share of the Community's claims costs
Each month, Bikelp SLP calculates Your proportionate contribution to the claims incurred across the Community during the preceding month. This share reflects the actual net claims paid on behalf of Community members and will vary month to month.

(b) Bikelp SLP management fee
Each month, You also pay a flat management fee to Bikelp SLP. This fee is charged under the provisions of this policy to remunerate Bikelp SLP for its duties as agent of the Insurer. Acting as agent, Bikelp SLP manages underwriting placement with the Insurer, administers policy adjustments, handles and settles claims, and maintains the Bikelp SLP Platform. The amount of this fee is detailed in Your Specific Terms.

4.2 Total Premium
The total monthly sum payable corresponds to Your share of Community claims, the Bikelp SLP management fee, and the statutory insurance premium tax. Your monthly Premium will never exceed the Capped Monthly Premium specified in Your Specific Terms, regardless of total claims volume incurred by the Community.

You will receive a monthly statement outlining the calculation breakdown of Your Premium for the billing period.

4.3 Premium Collection
On the third business day of every month, Bikelp SLP will collect the Premium due for the prior month. The charged amount reflects strictly the calendar days You were insured. Your monthly Premium will never exceed Your Capped Monthly Premium.

If direct collection fails, We will notify You of the default. You will be given 14 calendar days to settle the outstanding amount, failing which We may suspend coverage or terminate the policy retroactively to the final day of coverage for which payment was successfully collected.

If We have settled a claim arising during a period for which Premium remains unpaid, We reserve the right to recover the full value of that settlement from You.

We reserve the right to report payment defaults to relevant credit reference agencies.

5. Is there a cooling-off period?

You may cancel Your policy within 14 calendar days following policy inception and delivery of Your contractual documentation. If You have lodged a claim during this 14-day window, You will remain liable for the pro-rata Premium covering the days during which coverage was active.

To cancel Your insurance, please refer to clause 6.

6. How do you cancel the insurance?

You may cancel Your insurance by contacting Bikelp SLP through the Bikelp SLP Platform or by email.

When You take out a new policy or adjust Your Plan via the Bikelp SLP Platform, a mandatory 30-day lock-in period may apply, during which cancellation is unavailable—You will be informed of any such lock-in period before it takes effect on the platform.

Following this initial period, You may cancel Your policy at any time via the Bikelp SLP Platform or by email. You will remain responsible for paying the pro-rata Premium for the days during which coverage was in force during the month of termination. Your final statement will be generated at the end of that billing month.

You benefit from a 14-day statutory cooling-off period starting from the effective date of the policy and delivery of Your documentation. If You choose to renounce coverage during this period, You may do so free of charge, unless a claim has been filed, in which case the Premium for that period remains due.

Bikelp SLP may revise the terms of Your policy subject to one month's advance notice. Should You decline to renew under the amended terms, You may cancel the policy by notifying Us.

If You cease to be the legal owner of the Insured Equipment, You may terminate the policy at any time by notifying Us.

You remain liable for all accrued Premium covering the period during which You were insured.

10. What are your obligations when filing a claim?

When reporting a claim, You must:

  • Notify Us of the incident promptly, and no later than 28 calendar days following the occurrence.
  • Submit satisfactory proof demonstrating Your ownership of the Insured Equipment.
  • Cooperate fully with Us by providing all necessary supporting records, including verification of Lock compliance upon request.
  • Take all reasonable precautions to mitigate the loss and refrain from actions detrimental to Our legal position.
  • Report any criminal incident to the police within reasonable time and obtain an official crime reference number.
  • Promptly advise Us if Insured Equipment is recovered after a theft settlement has been concluded.
  • Disclose whether the loss is covered concurrently under any other insurance contract.
  • Obtain Our prior approval before incurring diagnostic, investigative, or repair costs.
  • Ensure all information provided is accurate, truthful, and complete.

Failure to adhere to these requirements may result in the reduction or total repudiation of Your claim settlement.

11. How will we settle your claim?

We will arrange for the repair or new-for-old replacement of the Insured Equipment or, at Our option, issue a cash settlement directly to You or directly to an authorised trade supplier.

The maximum indemnity payable shall not exceed the insured value specified in Your policy schedule.

You must settle any applicable Deductible prior to Us concluding a claim through direct supplier disbursement.

Where We settle a claim in cash, the maximum payout corresponds to the cost to replace the item of Insured Equipment like-for-like with a new item at the date of loss, subject to the policy limits in Your Specific Terms. Where cash settlements are approved, payment will be transferred within 5 business days.

Following claim settlement, it remains Your responsibility to amend or cancel Your policy schedule accordingly.

11.1. Under-insurance
If the declared sum insured on any item of Insured Equipment is lower than its true new-for-old replacement cost at the date of claim, We will apply the principle of average, reducing the payout in proportion to the difference between the insured amount and the full replacement value.

11.2. Salvage / Subrogation and Recovery
Upon payment of a total loss indemnity, We reserve the legal right of ownership over the salvaged or recovered Insured Equipment, or to exercise subrogation rights against responsible third parties.

15. What does the insurance cover?

You are insured exclusively for those specific coverage "modules" designated as "covered" in Your Specific Terms. Please consult Your Specific Terms to verify which options apply to Your contract.

Additionally, We will reimburse reasonable and necessary ancillary expenses directly incurred in resolving an approved claim, including independent assessment, workshop labour, and postage/freight charges.

Furthermore, We cover replacement bike hire fees up to the limits stated in Your Specific Terms, provided such hire costs are reasonably incurred whilst awaiting settlement of an approved claim under this policy.

15.1. Accidental damage module
We cover physical Damage to Your Insured Equipment that prevents its normal operation or compromises its structural safety.
The following are excluded from this module:

  • Damage sustained during a Race or a Professional Race.

15.2. Home theft module
We cover theft of Your Insured Equipment from inside Your Home, provided the premises were secured and locked.
We cover physical Damage to Your Insured Equipment resulting from an attempted burglary at Your Home, provided the property was locked.

15.3. Away from home theft module
We cover theft of Your Insured Equipment occurring away from Your Home; however, whenever left unattended, Your Bicycle must be secured to an immovable object with an approved Lock in accordance with the specifications in Your Specific Terms.
We cover Damage sustained by Your Insured Equipment resulting from an attempted theft away from Your Home.
If Your Bicycle is left Abandoned for longer than the maximum unattended duration permitted under Your Specific Terms, no indemnity will be granted under this section (Module 15.3 Theft away from home).
We cover the accidental loss of Your Insured Equipment while entrusted to a third-party commercial bailee (licensed airline, recognised courier, or certified transport operator), provided You establish that the carrier had assumed contractual custody.

15.4. Emergency travel expenses module
We will reimburse, up to the maximum emergency travel limit stated in Your Specific Terms, reasonable onward transit expenses You incur to complete an ongoing journey by bicycle when that journey is abruptly cut short following an incident giving rise to an approved claim under sections 15.1 or 15.3.

15.5. Racing damage coverage module
We cover physical Damage sustained by Your Insured Equipment while participating in a Race.
We do not cover any Damage sustained during a Professional Race.

16. What is not covered by the insurance?

We do not cover any claim:

  • Reported to Us more than 28 calendar days after the incident date.
  • Where You fail to produce conclusive Proof of Ownership for the claimed items, or proof of an approved Lock where requested.
  • Arising from the commercial or professional use of Your Insured Equipment.
  • Arising from the use of Your Insured Equipment in a professional race.
  • Resulting directly or indirectly from any criminal act committed by You.
  • Caused by deliberate, willful, or reckless misconduct on Your part.
  • Resulting from the Insured Equipment being Abandoned.
  • Relating to Cosmetic Damage.
  • Concerning Damage:
    • occurring during a Professional Race;
    • resulting from progressive deterioration, gradual wear and tear, corrosion, or ordinary use;
    • arising from Cyber Damage, mechanical failure, or electrical malfunction;
    • consisting of routine tyre punctures, cuts, or blowouts incurred during normal use.
  • Concerning theft:
    • Away from Your Home, where the Bicycle was not secured using an approved Lock adhering to the criteria in Your Specific Terms, and You cannot provide satisfactory proof of lock ownership upon request.
    • At Your Home where there is no visible evidence of forcible and violent entry.
  • Attributable to nuclear reaction, ionizing radiation, flood, earthquake, or volcanic eruption.
  • Arising from acts of terrorism or sabotage.
  • Resulting from War, civil unrest, martial law, or armed hostilities.
  • For theft or Damage taking place outside the approved territorial limits set out in Your Specific Terms.
